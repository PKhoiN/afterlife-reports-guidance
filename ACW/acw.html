A Cursed World

Find yourself throughout your deaths.

For noting purposes. A Cursed World will not be developed until 2030


Terminology

A "cursed" is an individual who has at least one curse.


Cycle of life

A cursed can resurrect a recently dead human, giving them another shot at life, a curse that reduces the quality of that new life, and the ability to resurrect once.

If a cursed was resurrected, they would get another curse, and the curses stack throughout their entire existence.

Only one cursed has the ability to resurrect at all times per region. If a cursed dies with an unused resurrection, then that ability gets transferred to another cursed within the same region.


Curse tiers

A curse can be in one of three tiers: Novus, Relief, and Supernova.

Novus: Assigned to be the tier for the first curse of a newly resurrected cursed. Usually life-changing and defines the entire future of the cursed.

Relief: Assigned to be the tier for subsequent curses. Usually have less impact than the Novus curse.

Supernova: Assigned to be the tier for curses that the Afterlife wants the cursed to be dead.
