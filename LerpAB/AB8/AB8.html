[Setting: City. A is on the roads. B is somewhere]

B: Pick me up

A: Sure, where do you live?

B: I'm not trusting you, so I am locating you to give you directions

A: Sure


A: Where do I go?

B: Turn right at your nearest 4-way intersection


A: *Makes a U-turn straight to the wrong way*

B: That is a 3-way intersection. Turn back


A: *Rotates the car 180 degrees, then drives on the wrong lane*

B: Dude, change lanes. You are driving the wrong lane.


A: *Changes lanes and moves in the right direction*


[10 minutes later]

B: Move faster. You are moving at 10 km/h

A: Fine. *Moves at 11 km/h*

B: ...*sighs*

[1 hour later]


B: You are at a 4-way intersection. I need you to turn right and move forward


A: *Rotates the car 90 degrees. Then drives into a building*

B: ...you know what. Drive through buildings until you find me


[2 hours later]

A: *Brakes*. Finally, found you

B: ...I'm good. I'll call someone else

A: Why do you live in a forest?

B: I don't. You drove into every tree in the city

A: Forests have trees, right?

B: ...just leave
