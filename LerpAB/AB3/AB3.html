[Setting: City]

B non-caffeinatedly walked out of a coffee shop on the sidewalk, unbothered by the unknown presence of A following them by walking the same sidewalk.

B turned a hard left near a corner. A turned a hard left inside a 4-way junction, then proceeded to get hit by a flying car. Luckily, the car wasn't fine, and A continued.

Suddenly, A tripped (not slipped) on a banana peel since it was a century old and had been turned into a rock. But A managed to fall gracefully and create a giant dent in the sidewalk. A then got up and moved on by still following B.

A turned a soft left for no reason.

A turned a soft right to undo the mistake of walking the wrong direction when trying to follow someone, but accidentally turned 60 degrees (or 140 degrees Fahrenheit), creating their favorite polygon called the 3 decadegree, 0.6 hectodegree, and 0.09 kilodegree triangle (or the 30-60-90 to be a nerd). By doing this, A walked in a circle, or a triangle.

B walked through a red light. A hit the traffic light on the head because it was blindingly red. The traffic light fell over, creating a permanent red light, or in this case, a giant metal beam on the road.

Finally, B realized that A was following them by looking back and said, "Why are you following me?" A replied with the worst word choice, "Because your city walking skills are unmatched. Also, I did not follow you for the past 2 minutes."

A then got hit by a car. Tragically, the totally pre-un-totalled car totally didn't un-not totally survive and wasn't not totally turned into a totally non-partial fully completely totalled tolled car.
