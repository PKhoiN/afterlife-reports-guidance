Cast: A and B. Written by PKhoiN
Style: Script

A and B were hearing the news. The news said that Tuesday was banned and that the wall would be demolished... today.

After running out of their house in excitement, they decided to break down the wall themselves.

A used a nuke they found on the streets. B disarmed the nuke because that would cause another war.

A used an axe to chop down the wall. Unfortunately, the wall was made out of concrete. B was not foolish, as they used a chainsaw to saw down the wall. Unfortunately, B was also foolish, and the wall was not made out of trees.

Then suddenly, more people came. Then more. Then more. All of them were breaking the wall with them.

And then the news came that the news reported mistakenly said "today" instead of "Tuesday". Since the wall was supposedly planned to be demolished on Tuesday, yet Tuesday was banned, it was a perfect way to keep the wall up forever. But there were too many people to convince otherwise.

After 24 hours of hard labor by many. There was still more wall.

After another 24 hours. The wall finally fell, reuniting everyone with everyone on the other side.

As for A and B, they died in the crowd surge.

The end.
