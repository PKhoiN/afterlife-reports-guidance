Cast: A and B. Written by PKhoiN
Style: Transcript

[Setting: A and B are trapped inside a small room with a closed door]

A: *Knocks the door*

B: Dude, what are you knocking for?

A: I don't know. That is the first thing I thought of

B: Try doing something else

A: Guess I'll try opening it. *Tries to open the door, but it is locked*

B: ...that cannot be good for us

A: If only there was a key that was blatantly foreshadowed right in front of us


[There is a parcel on a table]

A: *Looks at the parcel*. Do you know whose parcel it is?

B: I don't care. Whoever left that is pretty stupid, since I can take it. *Takes the parcel and rips it open*

[There is a dustbin inside the parcel]

B: ...dustbin?

A: That is indeed a dustbin

B: I don't need this. You can have it if you'd like

A: Just put it on the table. I will get to it soon, probably, or later

B: Nah. *Hits the door with the dustbin*

*The dustbin disintegrates*

B: ...I was not expecting that


[There is a strawberry on the floor]

A: *Sees the strawberry*. Weird and ominous. I thought that strawberries come in packs. You know? Those plastic containers

B: *Takes the strawberry and eats it*

A: Dude... Anyway, how is it?

B: Gross. It has been sitting on the floor for days

A: You're welcome

B: What


[There is a sculpture on the table]

A: *Takes the sculpture and throws it at the door*

*The sculpture disintegrates*

B: What was that for? I thought that sculpture did nothing to you. Besides, we could admire it if it weren't for you throwing the sculpture away

A: You took my strawberry. Also, why are you suddenly defensive about a sculpture all of a sudden?

B: Just focus on the sculpture

A: No

B: That seems like an unhealthy response to me

A: That vegetable is unhealthy

B: Wait, what... also wait, what?

A: We are moving on early because you will probably die if we don't keep moving


[There is a sign on the wall]

A: *Reads the sign*

B: What did the sign disclose?

A: Just read it. It is not that hard

B: I'm lazy

A: Fine. It says it wants to become a miner, and also it wants to feel the darkness

B: *Takes the sign off the wall*. Well, I don't know why, but let's make that sign's dreams come true. *Smashes the sign on the floor*

*The sign phases through the floor and flies into the void without both of them*

A: ...did that suddenly phase through the concrete?

B: Just look at it. It is not that hard

A: Well, it is kind of hard, since according to the laws and foundations of reality, it is hard to look at something that isn't even there


[There is a lightbulb]

A: ...guess that is a feature now

B: Lights were always a feature

A: Not until now, when our eyes see it

B: ...so. Isn't this supposed to represent the sun?

A: If you follow a religion, possibly. If not, possibly, since thinking isn't limited by what we believe

B: Sure, cyborg of quotes

A: Hey!


[There is a bed]

A: I still need an apology after you called me a cyborg

B: In relation to what?

A: ...what are you talking about?


[There is an axe on the bed]

B: Anyway. *Picks up the axe*. Are you thinking what I am thinking?

A: No

B: Correct! *Breaks the wall with the axe, revealing a small room with a chest*

A: Whoa. You could say that was a "breakthrough"

B: Love your enthusiasm, but I don't like puns that come from you


[There is a chest in the small room]

A: *Opens the chest to reveal a million dollars*. Dude! There is a million dollars in here. I could probably finance a business with this

B: What business?

A: ...bed?

B: ...say your prayers because I am going to kill you with this axe-

A: *Takes the axe and throws it at the door*

*The axe disintegrates*

B: ...never mind

A: I mind


[There is a bouncy ball on the table]

A: *Grabs the bouncy ball*. This could be practically useful with its flexibility. *Throws it at the door*

*The ball rebounds off the door and hits A*

A: [On the floor, probably dying from nothing]

B: Dude

A: [Still on the floor]. Can you please be sympathetic and emotional for once?

B: I'm not your sweetheart nor your grandmother

A: *Gets up*. Fine, but only because of your resentment

B: I don't care


[There is a light switch on the wall]

B: *Turns the light switch off*

*Nothing happens*

A: The accuracy of your assumption is not in my approval

B: You try it for yourself then

A: *Rips the light switch off of the wall*

B: ...

A: You see? I predicted that the light switch would come off, and it did

B: Did you change your prediction mid-action?

A: ...your inquiry stinks


[There is a phone on the table]

A: *Grabs the phone but drops it because it is too heavy*

B: ...does that phone weigh a kilogram?

A: Heavier. Also, it is parallel to the ground by being face-down on the floor

B: I feel like that geometry is fundamental in seeing

A: Sure

B: *Grabs the phone and starts looking at its apps* ...*Sees the "teleport" app* ...you know what this app does?

A: I have never seen such a combination of letters in my life

B: ...it is just the word "teleport"

A: ...

B: You know English, right?

A: ...

B: Forget it. *Presses the app*

*The phone disappears*

A: ...why does everything keep disappearing?


[There is a clock on the wall]

B: *Takes the clock off of the wall and starts to pulverize it*

A: What are you doing?

B: Making carcinogenic chemicals

A: I don't think that clocks can give anyone cancer. Also, why are you making carcinogens?

B: My patience is starting to diminish since we have made no progress in getting out of this room!

A: ...actually, you are kind of right


[There is a model of a village on the table]

A: The civilians in this village are pretty bad at trying to move like a civilian

B: Stand back

A: *Stands back because they have no intention of dying*

B: *Grabs a piece of sharp glass from the broken clock and stabs the model*

A: ...do you need assistance?

B: No. *Throws the model into the door*

*The model disintegrates*

B: ...I swear there was a one hundredth of a one hundred percent chance that would work

A: I thought so too


[There is a whiteboard]

A: *Grabs a marker*. Okay, my rationale for getting out of here is because I want to, and the door is disturbing me

B: Then why did you grab the marker?

A: For fun. All to keep me sane for a bit longer. *Throws the marker at the door*

*The marker disintegrates*

B: Also, when did you decide to present your reason to get out? This is politically insane-

A: Hey. No politics in this premise

B: ...you're right. If only there was an immaculate way to get out

A: Was that your concession speech?

B: You said no politics!


[There is a key on the door that is turned to be locked]

A: ...

B: ...

A: We are extraordinarily stupid

B: *Back kicks the door*

*The door opens*

*Both leave*
