In a calm, serene meadow of grass and more grass, there lies B, walking on a graveyard of grass. Its formal name is a "dirt path". B wanders aimlessly along the path. This is because the path is absolutely bonkers and looks like someone tried to map out a drunk duck's path of waddles, not like there were any ducks to begin with.

B walks up to a flower. Its stem is as green as nothing, since it is too thin to see with B's terrible eyesight of 20/20. Its leaves are leaving the non-existent stem. Maybe it is due to the fact that it is as green as brown. Its petals are white as snow, if snow is like tar or fresh manure from a rat. B continues while trying to unsee that horrible mess of a floral flora.

B looks up 90 degrees vertically and promptly falls onto the wilting flower. The sky is blue, the clouds are blue, the space is blue. The suspended mist is too misty to see. There are no clouds to conclude. But then, everything went dark like a black hole in the sky. Then B's eyes started to hurt. B is looking straight at the sun. No wonder B has terrible 20/20 eyesight.

B walks up to a tiny rock, also known as a pebble, aka a small rock. The geological formation of this miniature mineral is impeccable. Its vertex figure is not defined since it has no vertex. Its perfect aura shines brightly inside a hallucinating person's head. Its almighty perfection of all of its features created a religion of the holy rock that worships special geology and terraced terrain to reach and call overzealous bishops of clay and gravel to convert everyone to the perfected religious religion. It may actually be a literal holy shit from a holy being, but ignore that. It is probably best not to sniff it since it could ruin the surprise. But it is just a stupid rock without any stupid feelings, so why should B care about this? Why should B care about anything about this stupid world? Nothing is worth caring about. B should commit-

B walks up to an unconverted and unconvinced river. The water is flowing like a wave crashing in with other waves. B waves back, and a tsunami hits B like a crashing wave. Under the river, B can feel the coarse sand, the coarse dirt, and the coarse rock squishing and floating between their fingers. Its colors were blue, blue, and blue, probably because B has terrible eyesight underwater. B swims upwards into the riverbed. It was not, in fact, "upwards". B swims "upwards" in reverse into the sky to shove air into their biological air-inhaler, and then swims to the semi-wet, semi-dry shore.

B looks over at the horizon. No wooden trunks, no wooden bunk beds, and no wooden arches. Even without all the wooden things, there are no hills, no mountains, no rocky formations yet to be worshiped. Even without geology, there is only a grass meadow, and B as well. B sits down on a patch of nice damp grass even though some say it is uncomfortable. Also, some say it is filled with bugs, but all they really say is silent silence since the bugs do not exist here.

B, sitting under air and over dirt, ponders if they are going to see this again. The fate and life of B are still undecided, so it will be left unresolved and uncertain, unconfident and doubtful about being confident and sure about the decisions of their fate, but everything ends here for now and/and forever.
