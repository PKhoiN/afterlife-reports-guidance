Cast: A and B. Written by PKhoiN

[Setting: Store. A is the buyer. B is the cashier]

A: *Drops 100 dollars worth of items*

B: How would you be paying that?

A: Do you allow bartering?

B: Sure, it is company policies

A: Great. *Gives a ceramic cup*

B: Are you sure this is worth 100 dollars?

A: I bought it for 100 dollars. But I am willing to say that it is worth 5 dollars

B: Anything else?


A: *Gives a detailed map of a lost city*

B: Are you sure this is worth 95 dollars?

A: Are you good with directions?

B: No

A: This is probably like 10 dollars

B: Anything else?


A: *Gives a stick*

B: ...are you sure this is worth 85 dollars?

A: It is signed by Mother Nature. So it is worth around 15 dollars

B: Anything else?

A: *Gives another stick*

B: ...are you sure this is worth 70 dollars?

A: It is signed by both Mother Nature and me. So it is worth around 10 dollars

B: Wow, it went down. Anything else?

A: *Gives a bloody knife*

B: Are you sure this is worth 60 dollars?

A: No. At most it is worth 20 dollars, but since it is covered in blood, I'm bumping it up to 25 dollars

B: Anything else?


A: *Gives a 1-meter cube of pure plutonium*

B: ...are you sure this is worth 35 dollars?

A: Yes

B: Are you sure... if you underprice things, we will hit you with a baseball bat

A: Why do you suspect that?

B: That cube is at least worth 79 billion dollars, and you are valuing it at 35 dollars

!Excercise: What is the current price of a 1-meter cube of pure plutonium using this equation: $ = (g/cm^3) * cm^3 * ($/g)!

A: ...no, it is worth 34 dollars

B: Anything else?


A: My gravity?

B: Lower gravity has a cost

A: Yeah, I'll sell my low gravity

B: Are you sure that is worth 1 dollar?

A: I think so

B: Great, thanks for your transaction

A: *Grabs that 100 dollars worth of items and collapses due to higher gravity*
