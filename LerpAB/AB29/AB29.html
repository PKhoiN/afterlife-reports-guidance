2025 version:

A: Hi

B: Good, how are you?

A: I didn't ask how you are, but welcome to my house

B: Wait, you have a house?

A: Apparently

*Walking through the house*

B: Hey, what is that painting

A: That's the painting of you dying

B: Cool, how much is that

A: None, I stole it

B: Where

A: My house

B: Cool

*Walks to the kitchen*

B: Do you have chips

A: No, but I have 200 corn kernels

B: How did you know that

A: I don't. Anyway, do you want corn

B: No *Stomach rumbling*, maybe

*A grabs corn from the oven*

*A throws the corn at the table with nothing to contain the corn*

B: You missed. Anyway, I want some forks to eat

A: Oh, I have a pack over here

*Grabs pack*

B: That doesn't look like a fork

A: Yeah, it is a spoon. More plastic for the price of a fork. How many do you want

B: All 29 of them. How many do you want

A: Negative one, maybe I'll use the back of it

B: I thought you used the handle

A: Not now

*Starts eating*
