[Setting: Mountain. A and B are facing away, back to back. B is holding a rapier]

A: Do you see anything?

B: No. No danger here

A: Come on. I thought we could sense danger from far away

B: I don't sense anything

A: ...wait, I do

B: *Turns around*, alright I- *Accidently stabs A*

A: I think that is the danger...

B: *Unholds the rapier*. Are you okay?

A: ...no, I-

*A passes out and falls on the ground, making the rapier stab even further*

B: *Grabs A up*

A: [Unconscious]

B: Oh no. What do I do

A: *Dies from blood loss*

B: ...no use in holding them up anymore. *Drops A*
