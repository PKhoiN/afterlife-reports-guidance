LerpAB Style Guide

Transcript:

First appeared in LerpAB1. It represents the story in a dialog-wise way.


Local format: <Name> (<Form>): <Dialog> where <Name> can be anything (usually short); <Form> is the form of the character, usually blank (the parentheses is also removed); <Dialog> is the content of the dialogue.

<Dialog> can also have actions and states.

*Actions* represent actions. [States] represent states.

Global format: Anywhere not with <Name>. All actions and states are global to the world.

"Word-" represents cut-offs. "Word..." or "..." or "...word" represents pausing, waiting, or trailing off.

!Text! represents extra text that is not present in the story but helps the reader like you.


Background art:

First appeared in LerpAB2. It denotes the report using the non-default white background and/or encloses a section of the report in another background.


Script:

First appeared in LerpAB3. It represents the story in full paragraphs or sentences in the past tense.

When switching from transcript to script, we use [Switch to script style]. When switching from script to transcript, we use "Switch to transcript style."


Diagram:

First appeared in LerpAB4. It denotes the report having diagrams, not to be confused with art.


Tape:

First appeared in LerpAB5. It denotes the report has some lines only visible, and the reader can choose to scroll up or down the visibility.


Dynamic:

First appeared in LerpAB5. It denotes that the story can change.


Text art:

First appeared in LerpAB6. It denotes the report having non-default text color, text weight, text alignment, text size, text shadow,...


Puzzle:

First appeared in LerpAB6. It denotes the story having hidden meanings, easter eggs, exercises, and quizzes.


Foreground art:

First appeared in LerpAB18. It denotes the report having some foreground elements.


Audio:

First appeared in LerpAB20. It denotes the report having audio.


Rewrite:

First appeared in LerpAB29. It denotes the report having some/all elements that are rewrites of other reports/stories from the writer and us. Both versions are shown.
