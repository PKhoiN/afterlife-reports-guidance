[Setting: Temple]

B was standing next to a big door that read "treasure here", but it was tightly locked by an indestructible lock.

B walked around the temple, realizing that their minds had been blocking the presence of 4 unopened doors.



B opened the first door to find a grassland. It had grass and was on land. The land was probably great for gardening, yet there was no diversity.

B walked into the grassland to find nothing. No trees, no mountains, no saxophones in sight. Not even clouds or the sun. They felt wind, they felt the sun, they felt at peace. But there was still the treasure waiting to be rightfully taken.

B walked around, seeing only grass and grass. B gave up and decided on the next door.




B opened the second door to find... darkness.

Then a flashlight rolled up to B's foot. B took the flashlight and started to walk inside.

It was dark, as usual. The floor was damp. The air was nicely damp. The rat was horribly damp.

B walked and walked and saw an empty flower pot on a pedestal. B was obviously confused, as flower pots weren't usually that valuable to be granted permission to sit on top of a pedestal. But maybe this was different.

B then left. On to the next door.




B opened the third door to find an office building.

It was sterile white. It had no aura. And worst of all, it had windows to more office space.

B walked around the office, feeling like they had a job all along. One desk had a bag of flower seeds. B opened it up to find a single seed.

Luckily, there weren't any people working in HR, so B just left without telling anyone.




B opened the fourth door to find a dock next to a river. There was a bucket tied to a rope for collecting water.

After 15 minutes of door opening, there was no lead in the end.

Then, there was a store on the dock. B walked up there to find that it was a gardening store. But it was empty, with nothing in it. Which gave B an idea.



B ran inside the second door, grabbed the pot, then left.

Then B took the pot into the first door, shoved it into the dirt, then left with a pot of soil.

Then B stole the flower seed from the third door, hoped that HR wouldn't mind, and threw it into the pot.

Then B took some water from the fourth door and dumped it into the pot.

The seed grew into a sapling.

After 40 minutes, it bloomed.

B took the flower pot and placed it back on the pedestal in the second door. Then a secret compartment opened to reveal a key.

B took the key and tripped, sending it flying through the fourth door and into the river.

But an hour passed, and look who came back.
